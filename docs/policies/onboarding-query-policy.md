# NimbusWorks People Operations Policy

Version: 1.0  
Applies to: All employees, managers, contractors, and interns at NimbusWorks  
Effective date: 2026-05-02

## 1. Remote Work Location And Benefits

NimbusWorks is remote-first, but some benefits are tied to the employee's registered living location. Employees must keep their home working location updated in the HR system.

Location-based benefits include regional health coverage, commuter support, remote work tax handling, country-specific meal allowances, and statutory leave supplements. If an employee changes their registered living location, People Operations must reassess eligibility before the benefit continues.

An employee who moves from an eligible location to an ineligible location will not keep the location-specific benefit unless People Operations approves a written exception. The standard remote work stipend may continue if the employee remains employed in a supported hiring region.

Employees must notify People Operations at least 30 days before a planned international relocation. Unreported location changes may pause payroll adjustments, benefits, and local compliance support.

## 2. Hardware And Ergonomics

New core employees can choose one onboarding equipment option:

- Standard Tech Stipend: a GBP 500 credit for approved work equipment.
- Pro-Home Office Kit: a company-shipped package containing an ergonomic chair, external monitor, keyboard, mouse, and docking accessory.

Employees may not receive both the full stipend and the full Pro-Home Office Kit during the same onboarding cycle. If an employee selects the Pro-Home Office Kit, any unused stipend balance is reserved for replacement accessories only.

Finance must be notified before a stipend is paid. Workplace Operations must be notified before equipment is shipped. Address changes after equipment dispatch may delay delivery.

## 3. Knowledge Orientation

During onboarding, employees receive a project-specific Start Here pack. The pack may include relevant wiki pages, project documentation, stakeholder names, team rituals, recurring acronyms, and links to required tools.

The Start Here pack should be based on the employee's role, department, first assigned project, and manager-provided context. It should not include confidential information outside the employee's approved access level.

## 4. Social Graph And Culture Buddy Program

New employees may opt into the Culture Buddy program. A Culture Buddy is a senior employee outside the new hire's department who can help explain informal norms, communication style, and cross-functional context.

Culture Buddy pairings are optional. Employees may decline without affecting performance review, onboarding completion, or manager feedback. Buddy assignments should not be direct managers, direct reports, or members of the same immediate team.

## 5. Tool Access For Core Employees

Tool access must follow the principle of least privilege. Access should match the employee's role, department, and current project need.

Marketing employees may receive Viewer access to analytics dashboards by default. Editor access to the CMS requires either manager approval or an onboarding workflow that confirms the employee will publish or edit production content.

Design employees may receive Figma Viewer or Commenter access by default. Editor access requires project assignment or design lead approval.

Engineering employees may receive repository access only after security training and manager approval. Production access is never granted automatically during onboarding.

## 6. Manager Executive BI Access

Managers may request Executive BI Suite access during onboarding. Access can be configured as Bird's Eye or Deep-Dive.

Bird's Eye access includes high-level KPIs, revenue summaries, headcount trends, hiring funnel summaries, budget summaries, and team health indicators.

Deep-Dive access includes individual performance metrics, granular spend, raw support ticket data, project-level delivery signals, and detailed headcount planning views. Deep-Dive access requires director-level approval when it includes personally identifiable employee performance data.

Managers may choose to receive a Monday Morning Briefing at 8:00 AM local time. The briefing summarizes key BI metrics so the manager does not need to log into the dashboard daily.

## 7. Approval Workflows And Autonomy Thresholds

Managers may configure an expense auto-approval threshold. Expenses at or below the configured threshold may be approved by the agent on the manager's behalf if the expense category is allowed and no fraud or policy risk is detected.

The default maximum auto-approval threshold is GBP 250. Thresholds above GBP 500 require Finance approval. Any expense above the manager's configured threshold must be routed for manual review.

Hiring and headcount requests can follow one of two approval paths:

- Sole Approver: the manager receives all hiring and headcount requests directly.
- Department Lead First: requests route to the department lead first, then the manager receives only final sign-off requests.

Managers must set a Designated Delegate for emergency system approvals when they are out of office or unreachable. Delegates cannot approve their own access changes, compensation changes, or termination actions.

## 8. Notification Preferences And Meeting Buffers

Employees and managers can choose Real-Time notifications or batched summaries. Real-Time notifications should be used for urgent approvals, security events, payroll blockers, and time-sensitive onboarding issues.

Batched summaries may be sent every four hours for low-priority updates such as completed access grants, social introductions, reading list progress, and dashboard digest items.

Employees with several onboarding meetings may request protected buffer time. The default buffer is 15 minutes between intro meetings. Buffer protection can be overridden only by the employee, their manager, or a critical compliance meeting.

## 9. HR Query Answering Rules

The Agentic HR query assistant must answer employee questions using approved policy context. If the policy context does not contain enough information, the assistant must say that it does not have enough policy information and recommend contacting People Operations.

The assistant must not invent benefits, approvals, exceptions, legal interpretations, or payroll decisions. For ambiguous, sensitive, or high-impact questions, the assistant should provide the relevant policy summary and recommend human HR review.
